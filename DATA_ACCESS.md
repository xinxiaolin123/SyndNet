# Data access

## Released derived materials

`data/` contains release-specific de-identified case codes, reference labels, model probabilities, fixed predictions, model/seed/fold/cohort information, and individual-reader scores. `MRMC/01_input/` contains clean paired reader records, condition-level records, and recorded model predictions. Reader codes R01–R15 are pseudonyms; they are not reader names. Numeric publication exports are in `results/` and `MRMC/03_results/`.

These files are the approved derived materials in the frozen submission archive. They do not contain raw radiographs, DICOM images, original clinical spreadsheets, identifiable source workbooks, or imaging model checkpoints. Public pseudonymization does not independently guarantee that all linkage risks are eliminated.

## Materials not released

Raw radiographic data, annotations, clinical source records, restricted study manifests, and training/inference checkpoints are not supplied. Access eligibility, institutional approval, consent conditions, and a contact/access mechanism are not documented in the supplied archive; no access promise, email address, or ethics identifier is invented. Authors must specify any lawful controlled-access route in the manuscript's Data Availability statement.

The Apache-2.0 software license does not establish consent, ownership, or access rights for unavailable clinical materials. Third-party and controlled-access materials are not relicensed by this repository.
